# Background check pre-release checklist

- [ ] Purpose, basis and consent are recorded
- [ ] Identity is confirmed by two independent indicators
- [ ] Every claimed statement has a status
- [ ] Corporate roles and links are taken from registers
- [ ] Courts, insolvency and regulatory actions are checked; criminal record only by lawful means
- [ ] Sanctions, PEP and adverse media screening done, list versions recorded
- [ ] For EDD, source of wealth and source of funds are checked
- [ ] The conclusion is separated from facts, alternatives and gaps are named
- [ ] Unnecessary personal data is not kept, a retention period is set
