# Source of wealth and source of funds questionnaire

Questions to the customer.

1. How the wealth was formed: main sources and periods (employment, business, sale of a stake, inheritance, investments, real estate, crypto-assets, gifts and loans).
2. For each source: amount, year, documents (contracts, returns, statements, certificates).
3. Where the money in this relationship or transaction comes from: the source account, the account holder, the supporting document.
4. Whether third parties take part (donors, creditors, partners): who they are and how they are connected.
5. Which countries and intermediaries take part in the chain.
6. Expected volume and nature of transactions.
7. Links to PEPs, sanctioned persons or high-risk countries.
